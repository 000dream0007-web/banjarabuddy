Who are we

We're बंजारा Buddy, a small travel crew based in Jaipur. बंजारा is the Hindi word for a wanderer, and that's how we like to travel: slowly, with good company and no rush.

We plan small-group trips, work with local guides who know the places they show you, and keep every itinerary easy enough to actually enjoy.

Why travel with us

Small groups
Small enough to share one vehicle and one meal table, so nobody waits and everyone has a say.
Local guides, not scripts
Every guide lives where they lead. They know which chai stall opens early and which road is shut for the festival.
Time to chill
We keep travel days under five hours and book two nights in most places, so you can unpack.
Lower-carbon by design
Trains and boats replace flights wherever a ground route exists. We publish the emissions of every itinerary.
